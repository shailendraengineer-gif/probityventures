About Probity Ventures

Probity Ventures Private Limited is a Capital Organization Platform established to identify, create, prototype, validate, refine, develop, structure, finance and scale innovative technologies into sustainable enterprises across emerging and high-growth sectors.

We seek to bridge the gap between innovation, commercialization and capital by bringing together entrepreneurs, investors, technology providers, lenders, EPC contractors, O&M specialists and strategic partners within a collaborative enterprise development ecosystem.

Our objective is to create sustainable enterprises capable of generating economic value, environmental responsibility and long-term societal progress.

Founder & Leadership

Shailendra Engineer

Mechanical Engineer and MBA (Finance) with over 27 years of professional experience and exposure across Manufacturing, Energy, Electric Mobility Services, Cold Supply Chain Infrastructure, Circular Economy, Financial Services and EPC sectors.

His professional journey spans enterprise creation, project execution, technology commercialization, infrastructure development, strategic partnerships, capital structuring and business transformation initiatives across multiple industries.

Having worked with both large multinational organizations and entrepreneurial ventures, he has developed a unique perspective on the integration of technology, execution capability and capital formation required to build sustainable enterprises.

Probity Ventures Private Limited was established as a Capital Organization Platform dedicated to identifying, creating, validating, structuring, financing and scaling innovative technologies into commercially viable enterprises capable of generating long-term economic value and societal impact.

Under his leadership, Probity Ventures seeks to bring together entrepreneurs, investors, technology providers, lenders, EPC contractors, O&M specialists and strategic partners within a collaborative ecosystem designed to accelerate enterprise development and sustainable growth.

His long-term vision is to democratize access to capital, technology and opportunity while contributing to economic development, environmental responsibility and long-term societal progress through disciplined enterprise creation and responsible capital allocation.
